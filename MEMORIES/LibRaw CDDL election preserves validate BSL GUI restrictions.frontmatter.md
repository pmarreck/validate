LibRaw is dual-licensed LGPL-2.1 or CDDL-1.0. validate statically links it, so
the project elects the CDDL-1.0 option and bundles its canonical text at
`LICENSES/CDDL-1.0.txt`. The election governs only the LibRaw component.

validate's own source remains under the top-level Business Source License 1.1.
Its Additional Use Grant independently prohibits third-party GUI, API, service,
plugin, and application use absent a commercial license. Third-party license
notices must state this boundary rather than imply that the CDDL election grants
rights in validate.
